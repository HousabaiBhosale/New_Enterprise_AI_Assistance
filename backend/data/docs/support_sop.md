# Customer Support Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 1. Ticket Prioritization
- **P1 (Critical):** Complete system outage. Response required within 30 minutes. Resolution within 4 hours.
- **P2 (High):** Major feature broken. Response required within 2 hours. Resolution within 24 hours.
- **P3 (Medium):** Minor bug or non-critical issue. Response required within 12 hours.
- **P4 (Low):** Feature requests or general inquiries. Response required within 48 hours.

## 2. Refund Policy
- Full refunds are issued if requested within 14 days of the initial purchase.
- No refunds are issued for partial months on monthly subscriptions.
- Pro-rated refunds on annual plans are only granted in exceptional circumstances (e.g., business closure) and require Manager approval.

## 3. Communication Guidelines
- Always address the customer by their first name.
- Empathize with their issue before providing a solution.
- If an issue needs to be escalated, clearly explain to the customer that it is being passed to the engineering team and provide an estimated timeline.

## 4. Account Deletion Requests
- When a user requests account deletion, verify their identity via a confirmation email.
- Account data is soft-deleted for 30 days before permanent erasure, complying with GDPR.
