# Human Resources Policy

## 1. Leave Policy
- **Annual Leave:** All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave:** Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Medical certificates are required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- **Maternity/Paternity Leave:** 16 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
- **Applying for Leave:** All leave requests must be submitted through the internal AI Assistant or HR portal at least 7 days in advance for annual leave.

## 2. Remote Work Policy
- Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
- Core working hours for remote workers are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.

## 3. Code of Conduct
- We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against harassment and discrimination.
- Professional attire is expected during client-facing meetings, even virtual ones.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement
- Travel expenses must be approved prior to booking.
- A per diem of $50 is allowed for meals during business travel.
- Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
