# Sales Playbook and Guidelines

## 1. Product Pricing
- **Enterprise Plan:** $500/month. Includes unlimited users, 24/7 support, and dedicated account manager.
- **Pro Plan:** $200/month. Includes up to 50 users and priority email support.
- **Basic Plan:** $50/month. Includes up to 5 users and standard email support.

## 2. Discount Policy
- Sales representatives are authorized to offer up to a 10% discount on annual subscriptions for Pro and Enterprise plans without manager approval.
- Discounts between 10% and 25% require approval from the Regional Sales Director.
- No discounts are allowed on the Basic Plan.

## 3. Lead Qualification (BANT Framework)
- **Budget:** Does the prospect have the financial resources?
- **Authority:** Is the contact the decision-maker?
- **Need:** Does our product solve a critical pain point for them?
- **Timeline:** Are they looking to purchase within the next 3 months?

## 4. Commission Structure
- 10% commission on the first year of any new Enterprise contract.
- 5% commission on Pro and Basic contracts.
- Bonuses are paid quarterly for exceeding sales targets by 20%.
